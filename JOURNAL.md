# 9/20/2026: Preliminary resarch
So I'm doing al the research behind such kind of keyboards. I don't want those janky split keyboads with a curved shape and a cursed key layout. Sort of like a classic keyboard but split in half. Apparently they are called Traditional Split Keyboards. The keyboard I don't want is the Ergonomic Split Keyboard. Also, I'm contemplating on whether to add a plate. I could use a pcb thing topper as a plate, or 3d print the plate. But we'll see about that. I'm probably going to use creamy thocky switches, but with low-profile keycaps because I don't want my fngers getting cramped trying to reach over other keys. Also, I'll use a normal MDA or something idk layout, the keys are flat and there won't be a like a curved tapering among the keyboard. No RGB though, I don't want to dabble with that. <img width="711" height="761" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/638e33a8-2f3d-47d8-ac2f-0cfa00f1663e" />
